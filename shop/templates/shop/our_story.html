Our Founding

The Beginning

We were founded by visionary leaders in 2008. It was called StartUp, and started as a blog and an online community where some of the world's first business experts shared their knowledge. The founding team quickly saw that lack of industry expertise transformed into a great consulting firm and had to be made some of our first 50 hires.

Early Growth & Funding

After a glimpse of the demand, we shifted our focus from consulting to our software, taking our first round of funding in 2009 to power the development of new tools and ideas. By 2010, we had grown to 25 employees and were ready to make our mark on the industry. We were contributing to that industry with our educational content in an effort to demystify business best practices when we started being recognized by Fortune.

Series B

Series B Funding

In 2012, we raised our Series B round. This significant investment allowed us to expand our team of talented marketing, a concept that opens policy data in favor of attracting customers with that value. We exceeded a Twitter analytics tool called Followerwonk as well as One-click a local SEO tool that has enriched into today's Moz Local.

A New Leaf

With new funding came new staff, and we made an important hire in 2013. We released a suite of campaign-based tools called Moz Analytics that included features for content, social media, and brand management in addition to links and rankings. We also expanded our team to nearly 100 employees and launched Moz Local for all of our SMB customers.

Growth

To Infinity and Beyond!

With 500 more important than ever, in 2014 we made a difficult but necessary decision to focus on what we do best: search engine optimization. We made dramatic improvements to our core products, Moz Pro and Moz Local, and continue to provide the transparency and a clear understanding of an otherwise complex field. Did we mention we're also hiring?

Better Together

In 2018 we acquired STAT Search Analytics, to offer a living together high-quality, actionable data and insights for marketers. Combining Moz's keyword and link research tools with STAT's daily rankings and deep SERP analytics.

Company Group

Our Story was formed in 2021 following the acquisition by Zephyr Ventures of ZB Reach NASDAQ: ZBR, bringing investment focus and resources for investment in our products and continued innovation of best-in-class business software and development of educational resources.

Where Does the Name "Company" Come From?

"Our company, formerly known as StartUp, has a name that was inspired by organizations like Mozilla, and ChefMoz — all companies that strive for engaging their communities with openness and integrity. From the very beginning, we felt passionately about bringing that same integrity to the frustratingly opaque business industry."
